📖 How to Order Your Custom Hamper

Creating your perfect gift hamper is easy! Follow these simple steps for a smooth ordering experience.

1
🎁

Build Your Hamper

Browse our collection and select the items you'd like to include.

Mix and match products to create your perfect gift hamper.

❌ Don't exceed the hamper capacity if a maximum item limit is mentioned.
2
🛒

Review Your Selection

Before continuing:

  • Check selected products
  • Verify quantities
  • Confirm prices
❌ Don't proceed without reviewing your cart.
3
📝

Enter Delivery Details

Fill in:

  • Full Name
  • Email
  • Phone Number
  • Delivery Address
  • City
  • State
  • Pincode
  • Delivery Date
  • Recipient Name (optional)
  • Gift Message (optional)
  • Special Instructions (optional)
❌ Incorrect details may delay your order.
4
🧾

Generate Invoice

Click Generate Invoice. The invoice will include:

  • Customer Details
  • Delivery Address
  • Selected Items
  • Product Images
  • Quantity
  • Prices
  • Grand Total
❌ Review everything before making payment.
5
💳

Make Payment

Scan the QR Code displayed on the invoice. Or use the provided UPI ID.

Complete the payment.

❌ Don't make partial payments.
❌ Only pay using the displayed UPI ID.
6
📸

Contact Us

After payment you can:

  • Download your invoice
  • Print your invoice
  • Contact us on Instagram
  • Share your invoice if needed
  • Discuss additional customization
❌ Mention your Invoice Number while contacting us.
7
🎀

Custom Requests

Want something unique? You can request:

  • Different ribbon colors
  • Theme-based hampers
  • Custom gift messages
  • Personalized items
  • Special decorations
  • Additional products

Mention these in the Special Instructions section or contact us on Instagram.
